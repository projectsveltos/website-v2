Security is a top priority in Kubernetes management, especially when clusters are located behind firewalls or in private networks. Sveltos now supports a "Pull Mode" that allows managed clusters to pull configurations from the management cluster, eliminating the need for inbound connections.

This post covers:
- The architecture of Sveltos Pull Mode.
- How to enable Pull Mode for your clusters.
- Benefits for edge and multi-site deployments.
